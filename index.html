Social & Solidarity Economy

Work that builds a better world.

Who we are

An alternative career collective.

WeV is shifting our labor from capitalist power into community-led solutions. We focus on values-based career navigation and impact hiring within the social and solidarity economy, connecting people who want their work to build a better world.

What drives us

Our values are our compass.

  • 01

    Community & Solidarity

    We value community and solidarity through consensus-based decision-making and non-hierarchical processes.

  • 02

    Well-being, Dignity & Decent Livelihood

    We champion values-based careers with living wages, benefits, and genuine work-life balance.

  • 03

    People & Planet before Profit

    We value human and social utility over capital, putting people and planet before profit.
